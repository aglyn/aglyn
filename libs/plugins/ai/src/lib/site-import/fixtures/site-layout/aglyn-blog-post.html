What 10, 25, 50 or 100 client sites cost per month

Webflow, Duda, Wix Studio and Framer against one Aglyn workspace, at the tier a client site can live on, from each vendor’s own pricing page.

Fixture Author · Oct 2026

Most agencies never add it up. A site plan here, a workspace seat there, a client who needed the CMS tier after all. This sheet adds it up: what a portfolio of 10, 25, 50 or 100 client sites costs per month on Webflow, Duda, Wix Studio and Framer, against one Aglyn workspace.

Every price was read from the vendor's own pricing page on October 1, 2026, in US dollars on yearly billing, before tax. The tier is the plan a client site can actually live on: Webflow Premium (Basic has no CMS), Framer Pro (Basic allows two CMS collections), Wix Studio Standard (Basic holds 1,500 CMS items), and Duda Agency, which includes four sites and charges $168 a year for each one after.

The headline numbers

Aglyn's row is the plan a buyer would pick, with extra sites at $20 where the count sits between plans: 10 sites on Business, 25 sites on Advanced, 50 sites on Advanced + 25 extra, 100 sites on Agency. Sites beyond a plan's included count are $20 a month each on every plan. Client sites carry no Aglyn branding on any paid plan; the Agency plan adds a white-labeled console and email, at $1,049 a month with 100 sites included.

Run your own numbers

The cost sheet page carries a calculator: type your client-site count, choose whether a client site needs a CMS, and compare against one platform or every platform on the sheet. The per-site number is the one to carry into your next proposal. For a brief with a team, traffic, data and a store, the pricing calculator prices it line by line.

What is not counted

Each vendor prices these separately, so they are left out rather than guessed: Webflow Workspace seats for your team, Framer editors at $20 a month each, Wix Studio Enterprise for multi-site management, and Duda's add-ons. Aglyn's row is the plan price before metered usage, too: each plan includes a monthly bandwidth allowance shared by every site in the workspace, and traffic past it is billed at the rate on the pricing page. If a vendor changes a price, this post is wrong until we re-read it, and the date above says how old the reading is.
