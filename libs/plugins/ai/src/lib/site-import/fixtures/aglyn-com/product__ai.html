Most AI builders hand you a finished website and dare you to change it. Aglyn AI hands you a draft — made from your own components, your layout and your theme — and every part of it opens in the editor you already use.

WHAT AGLYN AI BUILDS

A draft for most of what you make.

Pages, and whole small sites

Describe a page and it builds that page in your layout. Describe a business and it plans a small site — the pages, their addresses, a shared layout and a contact form — before it builds anything.

Layouts, templates, components

A header, navigation and footer as one layout. A page template for pages that differ only by content. A block that repeats built once as a reusable component.

Forms that are ready to collect

Describe a form and it arrives on the Forms page with its fields, the answers it requires, a marketing consent box when it asks for an email address, and where each submission goes.

Emails, campaigns and follow-ups

A draft email built from the email blocks, with three subject lines to choose between — or that design plus the draft campaign that would send it. In the CRM, a summary of where a record stands and a one-to-one email drafted into the composer.

Copy, themes, listings and products

Rewrite any text on the canvas, or write it from nothing. Describe a theme change and get a proposal in the theme editor's own controls. Have a page or product's search listing written, and a product's copy and catalog ideas proposed.

Your figures, and what to try next

Plain-words answers about your own numbers, with every figure traced to the row it came from. Where your plan includes A/B testing, two to four variants of the copy under test — and a result read back in words that names a winner only when the numbers support one.

DRAFTS ONLY

It builds. You publish.

Every generation writes a draft, or a new unpublished version of something you already have. It never flips a published version, never registers an address on your live site, never changes your navigation and never sends anything. Publishing stays yours, through the buttons you already use.

A plan before any work

Each build job first proposes what it will reuse, what it will create and why, and what each page will hold. Nothing is generated until you confirm it.

Built from what you already have

Your layout, your components, your forms, your theme's colors and spacing — checked against the building rules before you see it, and asked again when an answer breaks one.

It proposes; you decide

A job you don't like costs the credits it spent and a draft you delete. A proposal fills in the fields you would have typed — it never starts a test or moves a visitor. It can also simply read what you have: what a saved automation does, or why a run failed.

HOW IT WORKS

From one sentence to something you can edit.

01

Describe it

Open the page that lists what you want built — Pages, Layouts, Templates, Forms or Components — and choose Describe it beside its create button.

02

Read the plan

The job answers with a plan: what it will reuse, what it will create and why, and the pages it will build. Confirm it to build, or cancel and pay nothing more.

03

Edit, then publish

The draft opens in the editor you already use. Change anything, fill the gaps it marked in square brackets, and publish when you are ready.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions about Aglyn AI.

Straight answers on what is included, what is gated by plan, and how Aglyn AI helps.

What can Aglyn AI build?

Pages and whole small sites, sections, forms, product listings, emails and campaigns, plus copy rewrites, theme proposals, search listings and fixes for the SEO check.

Does it publish anything by itself?

No. Every generation is a draft or a new unpublished version. It never publishes, never changes navigation and never sends an email.

How much does it cost?

Each request costs credits, shown before and after it runs. Paid plans include a band of credits with an optional add-on; the pricing page lists the bands.

Is it included on the free plan?

Yes. Free workspaces get a small monthly allowance of Aglyn AI credits to try it; paid plans include a larger band with an optional add-on.

Can I edit what it builds?

Yes. Everything opens in the Besigner editor, where you change text in place, swap images, move sections and add new ones.

Can agencies use it for client sites?

Yes. Describe each client site from its brief, get a draft, finish it on the canvas and publish under that client's host from one console.

What is Aglyn?

Aglyn is a website builder with the store, forms, CRM, email, analytics and Aglyn AI built in. It is open source and free to start.
