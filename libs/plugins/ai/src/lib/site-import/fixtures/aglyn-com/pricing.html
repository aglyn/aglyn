METERED USAGE

What usage past your plan costs.

On Starter through Agency plans, page views, form submissions, and site storage past the amount your plan includes are metered at our infrastructure cost plus a 30% margin, after card fees. Video, audio, file downloads and other media served from our servers count 1.6× toward bandwidth, because serving them costs more than serving pages. You are not cut off when you go past a band — the overage is itemized on your invoice.

Pricing FAQ

Monthly or annual billing?

Both. Annual is billed once up front and saves up to ~35% versus paying monthly. Switch anytime from the console.

What happens if I exceed a plan limit?

Storage and API calls keep working: usage past your allowance is metered at the rates in the usage table and itemized on your invoice. AI credits past your included band are metered the same way, per 1,000, unless you set a stop or a monthly ceiling in Billing — and on Free they stop at the band. Sites, seats, and datasets stop at your limit until you buy an add-on or upgrade.

What does Aglyn AI cost?

Every paid plan includes a monthly band of AI credits, and every Aglyn AI action draws on it, generation included. For more, add an Aglyn AI tier to any paid plan, per workspace: Spark $9, Boost $19, Plus $39, Max $69, Ultra $99 or Studio $299 a month, adding 4,000 to 149,000 credits a month on top of the plan’s own; Enterprise by agreement. Credits past the band are billed per 1,000 at the rate in the usage table. If you’d rather it stopped, turn on Stop AI assist at the included band in Billing, or set a monthly dollar ceiling on overage. Free includes 300 credits a month and stops there — nothing is billed.

How do transaction fees work?

Payments run on Stripe. Aglyn adds a transaction fee on top of Stripe’s processing — 0–5% depending on plan and what you sell: physical goods on the lower rate, digital products and services (bookings included) on the higher one. It steps down as you move up — 1% on Scale, 0% from Advanced up.

Can I self-host instead of subscribing?

Yes. Aglyn is Apache-2.0 — run it yourself with Docker and your own Firebase project (see the self-hosting guide), or use the hosted platform and skip the ops.

Can I change or cancel anytime?

Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel whenever you like from the billing page. Upgrades and downgrades are prorated as a charge or credit on your next invoice, and a canceled plan stays active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

Have a promo code?

Apply it at checkout — promotion codes work on any paid plan and show up itemized on your invoice.

Do you offer SSO or an SLA?

SAML / OIDC single sign-on is available on Enterprise today — you set it up yourself in the console after proving your domain. Enterprise also includes dedicated support with a named manager and a 24–48 hour first response. We do not offer a contractual uptime SLA.

How much does it cost to build a website on Aglyn?

Nothing to build and publish on the free plan: one site, up to five pages, forms, media and AI credits. Your own domain, the store, bookings and the CRM come with Starter; the pricing page lists every plan.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It does not expire or downgrade. It is capped rather than billed: one site, five pages, 250 MB of storage and 2 GB of bandwidth a month.

What is Aglyn?

Aglyn is a website builder with the store, forms, CRM, email, analytics and Aglyn AI built in. It is open source and free to start.
