BEFORE YOU START

Questions people ask before they build a website.

Straight answers on what is free, what is gated by plan, and how Aglyn AI builds a website.

Can I build a website for free?

Yes. The free plan gives you one site on an aglyn.app address with up to five pages, forms, the media library and the Besigner builder, plus a monthly allowance of Aglyn AI credits. No credit card, and it is not a trial that expires.

How does Aglyn AI build a website?

You describe the site and who it is for. Aglyn AI proposes a plan — pages, navigation, a contact form and a palette drawn from your theme — and builds each page as a draft once you confirm. Every part is editable on the canvas, and nothing is published until you publish it.

Do I need to know how to code to build a website?

No. You build on a visual canvas by dragging sections and editing text in place. Developers can go further with the open-source platform and REST API, but nothing on the site requires it.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, from the Starter plan. Connect a domain you own and SSL is handled for you. The free plan publishes to an aglyn.app address.

Will my site work on phones and show up on Google?

Every page is responsive. SEO titles and descriptions, sitemaps, Search Console and Bing verification and an SEO check that scores each page are built in, and Aglyn AI can write a page's search listing for you.

Can I sell online or take bookings?

Yes. Commerce, digital downloads and appointment booking are part of the platform from Starter, paid out through Stripe; in-person POS joins at Pro.

Do I need plugins for commerce, forms or the CRM?

No. Commerce, forms and inbox, media, workflows and the CRM are part of the platform and run in the same console — Aglyn itself is built from the same products you get.

Which features are gated by plan?

Custom domains, commerce, workflows and the CRM start at Starter. Versioning, video uploads and per-page analytics at Pro. Subscriptions, the REST API and white-label as you move up.

What happens if I outgrow the free plan?

Move to Starter for your own domain, a store and the CRM. On paid plans you are never cut off: extra sites, seats, datasets and CRM records are metered at published per-unit prices.

Can I white-label it for clients?

On the Agency plan, published sites, the console your clients sign into and every transactional email carry your brand — including running the console on your own domain.

What is Aglyn?

Aglyn is a website builder with the store, forms, CRM, email, analytics and Aglyn AI built in. It is open source and free to start.
