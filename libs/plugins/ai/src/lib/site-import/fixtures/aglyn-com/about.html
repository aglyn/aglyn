ABOUT AGLYN

The website builder with a real platform behind it.

Aglyn is a website builder with the whole platform behind it: store, forms, CRM, email, analytics and Aglyn AI. It is open source and made in Austin, Texas, and it is the tool we always wanted: design the real thing, run the business behind it, and own the platform underneath.

Start Free

Why Aglyn exists.

The modern website runs on a pile of disconnected tools — one to design, one to sell, one for forms, one for media, one to automate. Each is fine on its own. Together they’re a tax you pay forever in glue code, integrations, and context-switching.

We think it should be one platform: design on a real canvas, with commerce, forms, media, workflows, and analytics already there when you need them. And because the web should stay open, Aglyn is Apache-2.0 — yours to read, self-host, and extend.
