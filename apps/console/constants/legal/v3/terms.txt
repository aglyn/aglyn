"I waive my rights under the Deceptive Trade Practices-Consumer Protection Act, Section 17.41 et seq., Business & Commerce Code, a law that gives consumers special rights and protections. After consultation with an attorney of my own selection, I voluntarily consent to this waiver."
You represent that (a) you are not in a significantly disparate bargaining position, and (b) you are represented by legal counsel of your own selection or otherwise qualify to waive protections under Section 17.42. This waiver applies only to the extent it is valid and enforceable under Texas law; it does not apply, and is not intended to apply, where prohibited.
