# Frequently asked questions

## Is Aglyn really no-code?

Yes. You design pages visually in the [Besigner](/building-sites/besigner/overview),
bind them to your data, and publish — no code required. Developers can go further
with [plugins](/developers/plugins/overview) and the [REST API](/api).

## What can I build for free?

Core site building is **free**. Advanced features — commerce, workflows,
webhooks, the REST API, and more — unlock on paid plans. Each docs page notes
what it needs; see [Billing & plans](/workspace-and-billing/billing-and-plans/overview).

## What is Aglyn AI?

It is the assistant, building instead of only answering. Describe a page, a
layout, a component, a form, an email, a campaign, product copy or a change to
your theme, and it builds it from what your site already has. It is an add-on
on any paid plan, and Free workspaces get a small monthly allowance to try it.
See [Aglyn AI](/ai/overview). It is
[rolling out](/ai/overview) and is not open in every workspace yet.

## Can Aglyn AI publish something by accident?

No. Everything it makes is a **draft** — a new page nobody can reach, an unsent
email, an unpublished version, a theme change waiting in the editor, an
automation switched off. It never publishes, never changes a live page in place
and never sends anything, so the worst a bad answer costs you is the credits it
spent and a draft you delete. See
[the draft rule](/ai/overview#drafts-only).

## What is an AI credit?

The unit everything AI does is measured in — an answer from the assistant, a
rewrite, a generated section, a whole site — drawn from one monthly pool per
workspace. What a request costs depends on how much work it is, and a job shows
its estimate before you confirm. Your plan includes a band each month, the
[Aglyn AI add-on](/workspace-and-billing/billing-and-plans/add-ons#aglyn-ai)
widens it, and you can stop at the band or set a ceiling on anything past it.
See [credits and caps](/ai/overview#credits-and-caps).

## How do I use my own domain?

Connect it with the self-service wizard — see
[Custom domains](/building-sites/custom-domains/overview). If verification is
stuck, check [Troubleshoot verification](/building-sites/custom-domains/troubleshooting).

## Can visitors have accounts on my site?

Yes — let visitors sign up, gate screens to members, and manage them from the
console. See [Member accounts](/guides/member-accounts).

## Can I access my data programmatically?

On the Business plan, yes — the [REST API](/api) exposes datasets and records,
contacts, sites, and form submissions, plus [webhooks](/api/integrations/webhooks).

## How is my site kept fast and secure?

Published sites are server-rendered, SEO-ready, and served over a CDN. See
[SEO](/building-sites/seo/overview) and
[Site protection](/building-sites/site-protection/overview).
