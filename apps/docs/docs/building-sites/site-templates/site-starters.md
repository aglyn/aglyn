# Site starters

:::caution Rolling out
Site starters from the marketplace are a **release-flagged feature** that is **not yet
available** on aglyn.com-hosted workspaces. Until it is switched on for your workspace,
**Create site** asks you to choose a style instead, as
[Create a site](../../getting-started/create-a-site.md) describes.
:::

A **site starter** is a ready-made website you create a new site from: its pages, the
shared header and footer every page sits in, and its theme. You pick one in **Create
site**, before the site exists, and the site is born with all of it, published.

## Choose your starter {#choose-your-starter}

1. Choose **Create site** on the **All Sites** page, or in the **site switcher** in the
   app bar.
2. Name your site and choose its address.
3. When you can use Aglyn AI, **Start your site** opens first: choose **Start from a
   starter site**. Otherwise **Create site** opens on **Choose your starter** straight
   away.
4. Pick a starter card. Type in **Search starters** to find one by name, or choose a
   kind of site (**Restaurant & café**, **Photography**, **Online store** and the rest)
   to see only those; **Every kind** shows them all again.
5. Choose **Create site**.

Nothing is chosen for you. No card is selected when the window opens, and **Create site**
stays unavailable until you pick one you can create from. Closing the window creates
nothing, and **Back** returns to **Start your site** as you left it.

## What a card shows {#what-a-card-shows}

Each card shows a picture of the starter's home page, its name and who made it, and:

- its price: **Free**, or the price you pay for it once;
- **Aglyn** on Aglyn's own starters;
- **AI** on an AI starter;
- the kind of site it is made for;
- how many pages it creates.

## Where starters come from {#where-starters-come-from}

- **Aglyn's styles come first**: one starter for each of the 21 styles **Create site**
  offers, with the same pictures. Picking one gives your site the starter pages in that
  style's look.
- **Then starters from the marketplace**, made by publishers. A publisher's starter is
  reviewed by Aglyn before anyone else can see it.

## Starters and AI starters {#starters-and-ai-starters}

Where **Start your site** is offered, **Choose your starter** has two tabs:

- **Starters** are copied into your new site as they are: their pages, their header and
  footer, and their theme.
- **AI starters** are a starting point for Aglyn AI's start, which writes the pages for
  your business. Pick one and choose **Continue**: the start's questions open filled in
  from it, its style and, where it has one, its brand color chosen, for you to change,
  and **Plan my site**
  creates the site. See
  [Start from an AI starter](../../ai/start-with-ai.md#start-from-an-ai-starter).

## A starter with a price {#a-starter-with-a-price}

A card with a price says so when you pick it, with **Buy and continue**. You pay right
there, in **Create site**:

1. Choose **Buy and continue**. The price, the sales tax, the total and the card that
   will be charged open under the cards. Nothing is charged yet.
2. Choose **Pay**. Your workspace's card on file is charged, and **You own this starter**
   appears beneath the cards. If your bank wants to confirm the payment, its own step
   opens first.
3. Choose **Create site**.

An AI starter with a price works the same way, with **Continue** in place of **Create
site**: its answers are shown only once it is yours.

If your workspace has no card on file, the form to add one opens in the same place, and
then one for a billing address if there is none: sales tax is calculated from it. Both
stay on file for the workspace under **Organization → Billing**, where you can change
them. If the card is declined, nothing is charged: the step says why, with a link to
Billing to change the card.

Paying charges your workspace's card, so it takes the **Manage billing** permission as
well as **Create sites**. Without it you still see the price, the sales tax and the total,
but there is no **Pay**, and the step says someone who manages billing can buy it. Once
they have, anyone who can create sites can use the starter.

The starter belongs to your workspace once it is paid for, so creating another site from
it later asks for nothing more. **Cancel** charges nothing, and **Buy and continue** is
there again. A starter you already own says **You own this starter** as soon as you pick
it.

The receipt is the invoice under **Organization → Billing → Invoices**. To ask for a
refund, contact support, as for any marketplace purchase.

## What your site gets {#what-your-site-gets}

- **Its pages**, published as soon as the site is created. The console says **Your site
  is live** and names the starter it was made from, with **View your site** and **Edit
  your pages**. Turn on **Start in preview** before you create it to keep the site to
  your team until you make it public.
- **The shared header and footer** every page you add renders inside.
- **Its theme**: a built-in theme with the starter's brand color and fonts, or the
  starter's own theme, which is added to your site's themes named after the starter.

A starter with more pages than your plan includes cannot be used. **Create site** says
how many pages it has and how many your plan includes, and you can pick another.

## Make it your own {#make-it-your-own}

Everything a starter creates is yours to change. Edit its pages under **Pages**, and pick
another theme or change the colors and fonts under **Setup → Theme**. Changing them never
changes the starter, and later changes to the starter never change your site.

## Who can use them {#who-can-use-them}

Choosing a starter takes the **Create sites** permission, the same one creating any site
does. Without it, **Create site** tells you so before anything is asked. Paying for one
also takes **Manage billing**, as [A starter with a price](#a-starter-with-a-price) says.

## Publishing your own {#publishing-your-own}

Any workspace whose plan includes selling on the marketplace can publish one of its sites
as a starter, free or with a price: **Admin → Backup & template → Publish as a starter**.
You choose whether it carries your site's built-in theme with its brand colors, or your
site's full theme. A new starter is reviewed before anyone else can see it.
[Publish as a starter](save-a-template.md#publish-as-a-starter) walks through it.

## In the Aglyn app {#in-the-aglyn-app}

**Create site** in the Aglyn app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android lists the same starters, and
nothing is chosen for you there either. A free starter, or one your workspace owns, creates your
site at once.

The app doesn't take payment for a starter and doesn't ask an AI starter's questions. A starter
with a price shows it with **Buy on the web**, and an AI starter shows **Continue on the web**. Either
one opens **Create site** in the console on that starter, where you finish. You may be asked to sign
in there first.

You can also publish from the app: **Site settings → Backup & template → Publish as a starter**,
or **A site starter** on its **Publish to the marketplace** page.
