# Looks and themes for AI sites

:::note Beta
**Aglyn AI is in beta.** It is available today on every plan that includes it,
and it is still changing as we improve it. Beta means you can use it now, while
what it builds and the steps on this page may still change.
:::

Every site [started with AI](./start-with-ai.md) gets a look of its own: a base theme,
colors, a pair of fonts, and the style of its buttons, cards and forms. The **style** you
choose decides the family that look comes from. The details are picked fresh for each
site, so two sites with the same style and the same answers still look different.

## Choose a style {#choose-a-style}

The **Style** step of **Start your site** offers these styles, each as a card with a
picture of an example home page of that kind, drawn in its style. **Pick for me**, first and chosen unless you
choose another, uses the style that what you said the site is suggests, and names it.

| Style | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Business & services** | A local business or service company. The fallback when nothing else fits. |
| **Home services & trades** | Roofers, plumbers, electricians, cleaners and other trades. |
| **Law, finance & consulting** | Professional practices. |
| **Health & wellness** | Clinics, therapists and practitioners. |
| **Restaurant & café** | Restaurants, cafés, bakeries and bars. |
| **Online store** | A shop that sells products. On a paid plan it adds [products](./start-with-ai.md#a-store-from-the-start). |
| **Portfolio** | Showing your work. |
| **Design studio & agency** | Studios and agencies. |
| **Photography** | Photographers. |
| **Blog & writing** | Reading-first pages. On a paid plan it adds [posts](./start-with-ai.md#a-blog-from-the-start). |
| **Events & weddings** | An event, a venue or a planner. |
| **Fitness & sports** | Gyms, trainers, teams and clubs. |
| **Yoga & mindfulness** | Studios and teachers. |
| **Beauty & salon** | Salons, barbers and spas. |
| **Real estate** | Agents and agencies. |
| **Courses & education** | Classes, tutors and schools. |
| **Nonprofit & community** | Charities, groups and clubs. |
| **Music & bands** | Musicians and bands. |
| **Personal & resume** | A personal site or a resume. |
| **Landing page** | One offer on one focused page. |
| **Coming soon** | A placeholder that collects interest. |

Each card says, in a line, what its look is like. Its picture is one variation of the
style; your site gets its own. A style also suggests the pages that kind
of site usually has, such as a menu for a restaurant or services for a trade.

## Each style's layout {#each-styles-layout}

A style also decides how its pages are laid out, so two styles never read as the same page
in different colors. Each style opens its pages in one of two ways of its own, and your site
gets one of the two:

- **A photo across the whole page**, with the words over it at the start (a trade, a
  nonprofit, a band) or centered in large type (a restaurant, a coming soon page).
- **A photo filling half the page**, edge to edge, with the words in the other half
  (health and wellness, fitness).
- **The words beside a photo** (business and services, courses and education), or beside
  **two overlapping photos** (an online store, an event, a salon).
- **A white card over a photo**, holding the words (law, finance and consulting, real
  estate).
- **Large type above a wide photo** (a design studio, a portfolio) or a **centered heading
  above a wide photo** (a blog, yoga, a landing page).
- **A round portrait above the words** (a personal page).

The style also sets how its header is arranged, how its lists and cards are drawn (cards,
lines under thin rules, or icons), whether its sections of text take a photo beside them,
and whether a heading sits beside its items. The words, the sections and their order are
still written for your business.

## How the look is made {#how-the-look-is-made}

The style sets the range the look is chosen within: which base themes suit it, the range of
colors, how strong the palette is, the page backgrounds, the font pairings, how rounded the
corners are, the style of the buttons, the size of the headings and how much space there
is. The **Designing your look** step then picks one look from that range for your site:

- **Unique each time.** Each job has its own variation, and a look that would repeat one
  of your workspace's other sites is drawn again.
- **Readable.** The colors are checked for contrast, so text stays readable on its
  background. A palette that fails the check falls back to the starter colors.
- **Moving.** Every page gets [animations](../building-sites/besigner/animations.md#pages-that-already-move)
  that suit the style: calm styles fade in gently, bold ones arrive with more
  energy, and portfolios zoom their pictures in. Choosing them costs no credits.
- **Cheap.** The look is one short step, a few credits of the start's total.

## Your theme afterwards {#your-theme-afterwards}

The look is saved as an ordinary theme: the base theme it started from, plus its changes.
**Setup → Theme** names the base, and you edit every color, font and spacing value there
as you would any theme. Every page, the header and footer, and the contact form use the
theme's values rather than fixed colors, so a change in the theme editor changes the whole
site.

A site whose owner has already changed its theme keeps that theme: a guided start never
replaces a theme you edited.

## Change the look later {#change-the-look-later}

To change the look with AI, describe the change in **Setup → Theme**, such as *warmer
colors and a serif for headings*, and review the proposal before you save it. See
[Change your site's theme with AI](./theme-assist.md).
