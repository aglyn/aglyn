# AI chat on your site

:::caution Rolling out
The AI chat on your site is a **release-flagged feature** that is **not yet available** on
every workspace. Until it is switched on for yours, the **AI chat** card does not appear in
your site's settings and no chat shows on your pages.
:::

:::info Plan availability
Included on **Starter** and up. Visitor chats use your workspace's
[AI credits](ai-credits.md), under a site-chat allotment of their own (see
[Credits and limits](#credits-and-limits)).
:::

The AI chat puts a chat button on your published site. A visitor asks a question and gets a
short answer written from **your own site**: your business details, your pages, your blog and
your products. Answers link to the page they came from, and a product the answer names shows as
a card with its photo and price.

## Set up the chat {#set-up-the-chat}

1. Open your site's **Setup** page.
2. Find the **AI chat** card and turn on **Show the AI chat on this site**.
3. Write a **Greeting**, the first line a visitor sees when they open the chat.
4. Optional: add up to four **Suggested questions**, shown as buttons under the greeting.
5. Optional: write an **Opening message**. It appears beside the chat button a few seconds
   after a visitor arrives, once per visitor, so you can mention an offer or an announcement.
6. Choose **What the chat may do** (below) and **Pages**, then press **Save**.

Changing these settings takes the **Manage site settings** permission, which site admins hold
by default. Your live pages update as soon as you save.

## What the chat may do {#what-the-chat-may-do}

- **Answer questions** is always on. The chat answers from your business details (Setup →
  Business), your published pages and entries, and your products.
- **Add to the cart**: the chat can offer a product to add. It shows a card with the product
  and option; only the visitor's own press adds it to their cart.
- **Bookings and stays**: the chat can offer open times for a service, or check dates for a
  stay, and start the booking or reservation with those details filled in. The visitor confirms
  and pays at your normal checkout.

The chat never pays, books, reserves or changes anything by itself, and it cannot see orders,
payments or customer accounts.

## Pages {#pages}

Choose **Every page**, **Only these pages** or **Every page except these**, and list page
addresses one per line, such as `/contact`. End an address with `/*` for a whole section, such
as `/shop/*`.

## Conversations {#conversations}

Press **Conversations** on the AI chat card to read what visitors asked and what the chat
answered, newest first. A conversation where the visitor asked for a person shows **Wants a
reply**, with the email and question they left, so you can answer them. Conversations are kept
for 90 days.

The card also shows this month's figures for the site: questions answered, conversations,
cards offered to add or book, and credits used.

## Credits and limits {#credits-and-limits}

Visitor chats use your workspace's AI credits, held under a site-chat allotment so a busy site
cannot use up what your team's AI assistant runs on:

| Plan | Site-chat credits a month |
| -- | -- |
| Starter | 1,000 |
| Pro | 5,000 |
| Enterprise | 20,000 |

You can set a lower **Monthly cap** for each site. When a site reaches its cap, or the
workspace reaches its allotment, the chat tells visitors to call or email you until the next
month. A single answer usually uses a few credits.

Each visitor, each conversation and each site also has a limit on how many questions it can
ask in a minute or a day, so nobody can run up your credits.

## How the chat loads {#how-the-chat-loads}

The chat button appears once the page has finished loading, so it never slows your pages. The
chat itself loads only when a visitor opens it. It runs on your own site, with no third-party
script.

## Privacy {#privacy}

What a visitor types, and the chat's answers, are sent to the AI provider that writes the
answers, the same provider your workspace's other Aglyn AI features use, and are stored with
your site for 90 days. The chat keeps a visitor's conversation in a cookie on your site, so a
visitor who comes back sees where they left off. See [AI and your data](ai-privacy.md).
