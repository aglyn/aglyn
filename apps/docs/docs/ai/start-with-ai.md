# Start a new site with AI

A site you have just created opens on its **Setup** page with **Start your site** over
it. **Start with AI** asks a few questions, then plans the site, writes every page,
gives it a look of its own and publishes it at its address. This page walks through
each question and what happens after you choose **Plan my site**.

To scaffold pages for a site that already exists, or for several sites at once, see
[Generate a website from a prompt](./generate-a-site.md).

## How do you want to start? {#how-do-you-want-to-start}

The first step offers two cards:

- **Start from the starter site** gives the site a ready-made home page with a header,
  footer and contact form, live at its address, for you to edit.
- **Start with AI** (marked **Recommended**) asks the questions below and builds the site
  for you. On the Free plan the card says how many pages a Free start makes.

**Skip**, the close control and the Escape key all leave the window. Before anything is
being planned, leaving gives the site the starter home page and nothing else, and the site
does not ask again. If you have typed or picked something, the window first asks
**Leave without your answers?**, because what you typed is not saved. Once the site is
being planned, closing only closes the window; the job carries on.

## Your business {#your-business}

**What kind of site are you creating?** is the one required answer. A few words is
enough, such as *a neighborhood dog groomer that takes bookings*, or pick one of the
suggestions under the field: a local service business, a restaurant or cafe, a
professional practice, a trades business, a shop, a nonprofit, a personal portfolio or an
event. Keep it under 120 characters.

What you write here picks the site's [style](./site-looks.md#choose-a-style), which
pages it usually has and what each page says.

**Who is it for?** is optional. It is who every page and every search listing is written
for, and it decides which questions the contact form asks. For example, *local dog owners
who want a regular groom booked online*.

Both answers also fill your site's [business profile](./business-profile.md), which every
later AI job for the site reads.

## Style {#style}

The **Style** cards decide how the site looks and which pages it usually has: an online
store, a portfolio, a restaurant, a blog and so on. One is picked for you from your first
answer; choose another to change it. Each style gives the site its own variation, so two
sites with the same style still look different. See
[Looks and themes for AI sites](./site-looks.md).

## Details {#details}

- **Where do form submissions go?** decides where messages from the site's contact form
  land:
  - **The Inbox** (the default): every message is one to read and reply to.
  - **The Inbox, and CRM as a lead**: every message with an email address is also a lead
    to follow up.

  You can change this on the form itself afterwards.
- **Pages** is how many pages to write: 4 to 8 on a paid plan (5 unless you change it),
  1 or 2 on the Free plan.
- **Welcome email** (paid plans): **Draft one** writes a welcome email for the people who
  get in touch through the site, saying what became of their message. It is a draft
  design. Nothing sends it until you put it in an automation or a campaign. Free starts
  do not draft one.

On a paid plan a **Model** switch sits beside **Plan my site**. Leave it on **Auto**
unless you have a reason to choose; see
[Choosing a model](./ai-allotments.md#choosing-a-model).

## What it will cost {#what-it-will-cost}

The bar at the bottom shows what the start will cost before you begin, as *About N
credits (up to M)*: about what starts like it cost on real builds, and the most it can cost.
On the Free plan it also shows how many of your free credits are left this month and when
they renew. If the start will likely use more than you have left, the window says so and
offers **Build what fits** (it builds what your credits cover, then pauses until you
**Resume**), **Build the home page first** (a one-page start), and **Upgrade**. **Plan my
site** waits until you choose. See [Before a job starts](./ai-credits.md#before-a-job-starts).

## What gets built {#what-gets-built}

**Plan my site** starts the job straight away. A guided start has no plan to approve. It
builds, in this order:

1. **A look**: a base theme, colors, fonts and the style of buttons, cards and forms, made
   for your kind of site. See [Looks and themes](./site-looks.md).
2. **A header and footer**, as the layout every page sits in, with a link to each page.
3. **A contact form**, saved on the Forms page, routed the way you chose, and placed on the
   page that needs it.
4. **Your first posts or products**, on paid plans, when the site is a blog or a store.
   See [A store from the start](#a-store-from-the-start) and
   [A blog from the start](#a-blog-from-the-start).
5. **Your pages**, each with its sections, its address and its own search title and
   description. A home page opens with a hero and has several bands below it, such as
   services, about, social proof and a closing call to action. Photos come from your media
   library or a [stock photo](./stock-photos.md).
6. **A welcome email draft**, if you asked for one.
7. **Publishing**: see [Published, and found by search engines](#published-and-indexable).

Contact details are never made up. A phone number, address or opening hours appear only
when you have entered them in your site settings.

## Building your site {#building-your-site}

**Plan my site** takes you to the site's **Building your site** page. It shows one row per
step as it happens:

- **Planning your pages**
- **Designing your look**
- **Building the header and footer**
- **Building your contact form**
- **Writing your first posts** or **Adding your first products** (a paid blog or store)
- **Writing your pages**, then **Writing page 2 of 5: About** as each page is written
- **Writing your welcome email**
- **Publishing your site**

Each row reads **In progress**, **Waiting**, **Done**, **Paused**, **Not built** or
**Couldn't be built**, and a finished row shows the credits it used. **Credits used so
far** adds them up. **What we're building** opens the plan: every page and its sections.
If one part fails, its row says so and the rest of the site keeps building.

You can leave the page: the job keeps going, the notification you get when it finishes
opens the page again, and the **AI** chip in the top bar says what it is doing.

When it is done the page says **Your site is live**, with the credits the site used,
**View your site**, which opens it in a new tab, and **Edit your pages**. If some pages
were not built, it says how many were and offers **Try again what failed**, which builds
only those.

## Paused, or out of credits {#paused-or-out-of-credits}

When the workspace runs out of AI credits partway through, the job pauses instead of
failing, and the page says **Your site is paused**. Nothing is lost: what it built so far
is saved. Once there are credits again, from **Get more AI credits** or when your monthly
credits reset, choose **Resume**. If you do not, it carries on by itself within about an
hour of credits being available.

## If it does not work out {#try-again}

If the site cannot be built, the page says **Your site was not built** and why.

- **Try again** reopens the questions with your answers filled in, so you can adjust them
  and start a fresh job.
- **Use the starter site instead** gives the site the starter home page. It is offered only
  while nothing has been built.

When a job fails because of something on our side, the credits it used are given back:
all of them if it built nothing, or the failed step's if it had already built some pages.
The page says so. A job you cancel yourself is not given back. See
[Credits given back](./ai-credits.md#credits-given-back).

## Published, and found by search engines {#published-and-indexable}

A guided start **publishes** the site when it finishes, so it is live at its address:

- the new home page takes the site's address, and an untouched starter home page is moved
  to the trash;
- each page gets an entry in the header's navigation;
- a page that cannot be published stays a draft, and **Building your site** names it with
  the reason, for you to fix in **Edit your pages** and publish.

The site is **open to search engines from the start**. A guided start never hides a site
from search. If you want to keep it out of search results for now, use the site's
[search indexing](../building-sites/seo/overview.md) setting.

Until the site is built, its address shows a short **coming soon** page with the site's
name, which search engines are asked not to index.

## The site's search listing {#the-sites-search-listing}

The site's own search title and description, the fallback for every page without its own,
are not saved for you. The site's **SEO** section shows them in a card, **The listing your
answers describe**. **Put in the form** stages them in the SEO form below as unsaved
changes, and the form's **Update** saves them.

## A store from the start {#a-store-from-the-start}

On a paid plan, a site with the **Online store** style also gets three to six **products**,
each with a description and a photo, and a **Cart** button in the header. Each product gets
a placeholder starting price so it can be bought; set its real price, and your own photo, in
**Products**. A product without a price shows **Price coming soon** on the store, with no
**Add to cart**, until you give it one. See [Product copy and catalogs with
AI](./products-with-ai.md).

## A blog from the start {#a-blog-from-the-start}

On a paid plan, a site with the **Blog & writing** style also gets a **Blog** collection
with three posts, signed with the business name and published with the site. Each post
has a cover photo, and the blog's index page shows the covers on its cards. See
[Build a blog](../building-sites/site-templates/build-a-blog.md).

## On the Free plan {#on-the-free-plan}

A Free start builds one or two pages: the home page and the one page your business most
needs. It keeps the site within about eight sections in all, uses the same look, header,
footer and contact form as a paid start, and drafts no welcome email, posts or products.
It spends from the [300 AI credits](./ai-credits.md#free-plan) a Free workspace has each
month.

## Who can use it {#who-can-use-it}

**Start with AI** appears when your workspace has AI generation, you have the **Generate
with AI** permission, and AI is on for the site. Otherwise a new site opens on the starter
site. See [Who can use Aglyn AI](./overview.md#who-can-use-it).
