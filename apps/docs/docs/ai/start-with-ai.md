# Start a new site with AI

:::note Beta
**Aglyn AI is in beta.** It is available today on every plan that includes it,
and it is still changing as we improve it. Beta means you can use it now, while
what it builds and the steps on this page may still change.
:::

**Create site** opens **Start your site** straight away, before the site exists.
**Start with AI** asks a few questions, one per step, then creates the site, plans it,
writes every page, gives it a look of its own and publishes it at its address. This page
walks through each step and what happens after you choose **Plan my site**.

To scaffold pages for a site that already exists, or for several sites at once, see
[Generate a website from a prompt](./generate-a-site.md).

## How do you want to start? {#how-do-you-want-to-start}

The first step asks for the site's **Site name** and **Subdomain**, its free address
on Aglyn. The subdomain follows the name until you change it, and is checked as you
type: if it is taken, the field says so and offers available ones to pick. Then it
offers two cards:

- **Start from the starter site** gives the site a ready-made home page with a header,
  footer and contact form, live at its address, for you to edit.
- **Start with AI** (marked **Recommended**) asks the questions below and builds the site
  for you. On the Free plan the card says how many pages a Free start makes.

**Bring my site** is a third card for moving the website
you have now into Aglyn. See [Bring my site](./bring-my-site.md).

No site exists until you choose. **Start from the starter site** and **Skip** (in the
bar at the top) create the site with the starter home page and open it. **Plan my site**
creates the site and starts planning it. The close control and the Escape key create
nothing: the window closes and no site is made. If you have typed or picked something,
the window first asks **Leave without your answers?**, because what you typed is not
saved. Once the site is being planned, closing only closes the window; the job carries on.

If your role cannot create sites, or your plan has no site left, **Create site** says so
before asking anything. **Plan my site** checks that planning can start before it creates
the site. If it cannot, the window says why and no site is made. On the Free plan, a start
that may need more credits than you have left asks first, and the site is created only once
you choose. If planning still cannot start after the site is created, the window says why;
leaving then gives that site the starter home page, so it is never left empty.

## The questions, one at a time {#the-questions}

**Start with AI** asks one thing per step. The bar under the title shows where you are:
**Start**, **Your site**, **Audience**, **Style**, **Details** and **Review** (on a phone,
*Step 3 of 6* and a progress bar). **Next** moves on and **Back** goes back; your answers
are kept either way. Pressing Enter in a text answer is the same as **Next**. If a step
cannot move on, the reason shows under the field and the cursor goes to it. **Skip** and
the close control stay in the bar at the top on every step.

Every question step also has **More details**, a closed section under its answers with
optional extras that fit the step. Open it to tell the site more about your business, or
leave it closed. Everything in it can be changed later. If something in it cannot be used,
**Next** opens it and says why under the field.

## Your site {#your-business}

**What kind of site are you creating?** is the one required answer. A few words is
enough, such as *a neighborhood dog groomer that takes bookings*, or pick the closest
card under the field: **Local service business**, **Restaurant or cafe**, **Professional
practice**, **Trades business**, **Shop**, **Nonprofit**, **Personal portfolio** or
**Event**. A card fills in the field for you. Keep it under 120 characters.

![The Your site step of Start your site: the progress bar on Your site, the question What kind of site are you creating? answered with a neighborhood dog groomer, and eight cards to pick the closest kind instead, with Back and Next at the bottom](/img/ai/start-with-ai-kind.png)

What you write here picks the site's [style](./site-looks.md#choose-a-style), unless you
choose one yourself, and decides which pages it usually has and what each page says.

**More details** here: a **Tagline**, a short line about the business in your words, and
the **Year founded**.

## Audience {#audience}

**Who is it for?** is optional. It is who every page and every search listing is written
for, and it decides which questions the contact form asks. For example, *local dog owners
who want a regular groom booked online*.

Both answers also fill your site's [business profile](./business-profile.md), which every
later AI job for the site reads, and so do the areas you serve, from **More details**.

**More details** here: the **Areas you serve** (cities, towns or neighborhoods, one a
line), your **Price range** ($ to $$$$) and the **Languages you serve customers in**.

## Style {#style}

The **Style** step shows each style as a card with a picture: an example home page of
that kind of site, drawn in its style, each with a photo. A photographer's opens on a
gallery, a café's on its heading centered over a photo of the café with its menu under
it, an online store's on two photos of what it sells, a blog's on a centered heading
above a wide photo, an events site's on its dates beside two photos of the day and a real
estate site's on a card over a photo of a home, so you can see each style's colors,
fonts, buttons and corners, and the shape of its pages, before you pick. The picked card
is outlined in color and carries a check. See
[each style's layout](./site-looks.md#each-styles-layout).
**Pick for me** comes first and is chosen unless you choose another. It names the style
your first answer suggests, such as *From your answer: Home services & trades*. The style
decides how the site looks and which pages it usually has: an online store, a portfolio,
a restaurant, a blog and so on. Each site gets its own variation, so two sites with the
same style still look different, and yours will not match the picture exactly. See
[Looks and themes for AI sites](./site-looks.md).

**More details** here: a **Brand color**, picked from the colors shown. It becomes your
site's main color, kept readable everywhere it is used. **Let the style choose** leaves it
to the style.

![The Style step: Pick for me chosen, naming the style the answer suggests, beside cards for Business & services, Home services & trades and more, each with a picture of an example home page of that kind and a line about the look](/img/ai/start-with-ai-style.png)

### Sites you like {#sites-you-like}

**Sites you like** (rolling out, not yet available) is an optional block under the style
cards. Type the address of a website whose look you like, such as `https://example.com`,
in **Site 1**. A paid plan can name up to three sites. On the Free plan, **Site 2** and
**Site 3** are shown but cannot be used, with the reason and an **Upgrade** link.

Aglyn AI takes only how those sites are laid out and styled: the order of their sections,
their spacing and header, their colors, the mood of their type, their buttons, cards and
corners, and their motion. It never takes their words, photos or logos, and it uses
Aglyn's own themes and font pairings, so your site stays your own.

- The first site's section order leads your home page.
- With more than one site, each choice follows what most of them share, and the first site
  decides a tie.
- A **Brand color** you pick under **More details** wins over the sites' colors.
- On a paid plan, Aglyn AI also matches the features those sites offer, like booking, a
  store or a blog, with Aglyn's own parts, when your kind of site can have them.
- Only each site's home page is read, once, while your pages are planned.

The **Review** step lists them under **Sites you like**, by site name.

## Details {#details}

- **Business phone** and **Business email** come first. Both are optional. When you give
  them, your site shows them from the start: on the contact page, in the footer of every
  page and on the buttons that ask a visitor to call or write, as links that open the
  phone or the email app. Your site's search listing details and the information AI
  assistants read carry them too. Without them, no page shows a phone or email row, and
  nothing is made up.
- **Where do form submissions go?** decides where messages from the site's contact form
  land:
  - **The Inbox** (the default): every message is one to read and reply to.
  - **The Inbox, and CRM as a lead**: every message with an email address is also a lead
    to follow up.

  You can change this on the form itself afterwards.
- **Pages** is how many pages to write: 4 to 8 on a paid plan (5 unless you change it),
  1 or 2 on the Free plan.
- **Welcome email** (paid plans): **Draft one** writes a welcome email for the people who
  get in touch through the site, saying what became of their message. It is a draft
  design. Nothing sends it until you put it in an automation or a campaign. Free starts
  do not draft one.

**More details** here: the **Street address**, **City or town**, **State or region**,
**ZIP or postal code** and **Country**; the **Opening hours**, picked a day at a time (a
switch for each day, and its times from a list); and your **Social profiles**, one link a
line, such as *instagram.com/yourbusiness*.

## Review {#review}

The last step lists every answer: the kind of site, who it is for, the style, where form
submissions go, the pages and, on a paid plan, the welcome email, then every detail you
gave, such as the phone, the email, the address and the opening hours. **Edit** beside an
answer opens its step, and **Back to review** returns here. **Plan my site** starts the
site.

![The Review step: each answer with Edit beside it, and at the bottom the estimate, the model switch, Back and Plan my site](/img/ai/start-with-ai-review.png)

On a paid plan a **Model** switch sits beside **Plan my site**. Leave it on **Auto**
unless you have a reason to choose; see
[Choosing a model](./ai-allotments.md#choosing-a-model).

## What it will cost {#what-it-will-cost}

The bar at the bottom of the review shows what the start will cost before you begin, as *About N
credits (up to M)*: about what starts like it cost on real builds, and the most it can cost.
On the Free plan it also shows how many of your free credits are left this month and when
they renew. If the start will likely use more than you have left, the review says so and
offers **Build what fits** (it builds what your credits cover, then pauses until you
**Resume**), **Build the home page first** (a one-page start), and **Upgrade**. **Plan my
site** waits until you choose. See [Before a job starts](./ai-credits.md#before-a-job-starts).

## What gets built {#what-gets-built}

**Plan my site** starts the job straight away. A guided start has no plan to approve. It
builds, in this order:

1. **A look**: a base theme, colors, fonts and the style of buttons, cards and forms, made
   for your kind of site. See [Looks and themes](./site-looks.md).
2. **A header and footer**, as the layout every page sits in, with a link to each page. The
   footer shows the phone and email you gave, as links that call and write.
3. **A contact form**, saved on the Forms page, routed the way you chose, and placed on the
   page that needs it.
4. **Your first posts or products**, when the site is a blog or, on paid plans, a store.
   See [A store from the start](#a-store-from-the-start) and
   [A blog from the start](#a-blog-from-the-start).
   **Your first events**, when your business holds them and your workspace has the Event
   Calendar add-on. See [Events from the start](#events-from-the-start).
5. **Your pages**, each with its sections, its address and its own search title and
   description. A home page opens with a hero and has several bands below it, such as
   services, about, social proof and a closing call to action. Photos come from your media
   library or a [stock photo](./stock-photos.md), and every photo gets a description for
   screen readers and search engines. The page with your contact form also shows the
   phone and email you gave. A list a page shows, such as your packages or your
   questions and answers, is kept as a [dataset](../content-and-data/datasets/overview.md)
   on plans that include datasets. It is shared with this site only, so you edit it on the
   **Data** page, and it is deleted with the site if you delete the site. When your
   answers give no entries for a list, such as a race schedule with no dates, the list
   starts with **sample rows** that look complete, so the page does too. A dated list's
   samples fall on weekends over the coming season, and a list of past results gets past
   dates, so the two never show the same rows. On the **Data** page each sample row is
   marked **Sample**; replace it with your own, and every page that lists it follows.
   Saving a sample row makes it yours. Visitors never see the word "sample".
6. **A welcome email draft**, if you asked for one.
7. **Publishing**: see [Published, and found by search engines](#published-and-indexable).

Contact details are never made up. A phone number, email, address or opening hours appear
only when you gave them on the **Details** step or entered them in your site settings.

## Building your site {#building-your-site}

**Plan my site** takes you to the site's **Building your site** page. It shows one row per
step as it happens:

- **Planning your pages**
- **Designing your look**
- **Building the header and footer**
- **Building your contact form**
- **Writing your first posts** (**Writing your first post** on the Free plan) or **Adding
  your first products** (a blog, or a paid store)
- **Writing your pages**, then **Writing page 2 of 5: About** as each page is written
- **Adding your account, cart and policy pages** (a paid store)
- **Writing your welcome email**
- **Publishing your site**

Each row reads **In progress**, **Waiting**, **Done**, **Paused**, **Not built** or
**Couldn't be built**, and a finished row shows the credits it used. **Credits used so
far** adds them up. **What we're building** opens the plan: every page and its sections.
If one part fails, its row says so and the rest of the site keeps building.

You can leave the page: the job keeps going, the notification you get when it finishes —
which names the site and, by email, gives its address — opens the page again, and the **AI** chip in the top bar says what it is doing.

When it is done the page says **Your site is live**, with the credits the site used,
**View your site**, which opens it in a new tab, and **Edit your pages**. If some pages
were not built, it says how many were and offers **Try again what failed**, which builds
only those.

## Paused, or out of credits {#paused-or-out-of-credits}

When the workspace runs out of AI credits partway through, the job pauses instead of
failing, and the page says **Your site is paused**. Nothing is lost: what it built so far
is saved. Once there are credits again, from **Get more AI credits** or when your monthly
credits reset, choose **Resume**. If you do not, it carries on by itself within about an
hour of credits being available.

## If it does not work out {#try-again}

If the site cannot be built, the page says **Your site was not built** and why.

- **Try again** reopens the questions on the review, with your answers filled in, so you
  can adjust them and start a fresh job.
- **Use the starter site instead** gives the site the starter home page. It is offered only
  while nothing has been built.

When a job fails because of something on our side, the credits it used are given back:
all of them if it built nothing, or the failed step's if it had already built some pages.
The page says so. A job you cancel yourself is not given back. See
[Credits given back](./ai-credits.md#credits-given-back).

You can also stop the build yourself with **Cancel** on the **Building your site** page.
The pages it already made stay as drafts, nothing is published, and the page offers
**Start again** and **Use the starter site instead**. See
[Cancel a job](./ai-jobs-and-activity.md#cancel-a-new-site).

## Published, and found by search engines {#published-and-indexable}

A guided start **publishes** the site when it finishes, so it is live at its address:

- the new home page takes the site's address, and an untouched starter home page is moved
  to the trash;
- each page gets an entry in the header's navigation, and takes its place in **Pages** in
  the order the plan lists it, which the
  [built-in error page](../building-sites/site-protection/error-screens.md)'s links follow too;
- every picture, and every dataset record's photo, still showing a starter photo first
  gets a photo of your own site: the start asks Pixabay again for any photo it could not
  copy, then uses the photos it copied for your pages (see
  [Stock photos in AI sites](./stock-photos.md#where-the-photos-come-from));
- the site's **Social image** becomes your home page's main photo, unless you chose one
  (see [Your site's Social image](./stock-photos.md#social-image));
- a page that cannot be published stays a draft, and **Building your site** names it with
  the reason, for you to fix in **Edit your pages** and publish;
- a page that **Couldn't be built**, or stayed a draft, is linked from nowhere on the
  published site: its header and footer entries, and the buttons on other pages that went
  to it, are taken out, so no visitor follows a link to a missing page. **Try again what
  failed** adds the page's header entry back once it is built; a button you want on
  another page, add in the Besigner.

The site is **open to search engines from the start**. A guided start never hides a site
from search. If you want to keep it out of search results for now, use the site's
[search indexing](../building-sites/seo/overview.md) setting.

Until the site is built, its address shows a short **coming soon** page with the site's
name, which search engines are asked not to index.

## The site's search listing {#the-sites-search-listing}

The site's own search title and description are the fallback for every page without its
own, and what search results and AI assistants read about the site as a whole. When the
site is published, your answers become them, led by the site's name, as long as the site
still has the title and description it was created with (its name and *Welcome to …*).

A title or description you changed yourself is never replaced. Instead, the site's **SEO**
section shows the start's in a card, **The listing your answers describe**. **Put in the
form** stages them in the SEO form below as unsaved changes, and the form's **Update** saves
them.

## Your business, for search engines and AI assistants {#business-details}

When the site is published, your answers also fill the site's
[structured data](../building-sites/seo/overview.md#entity-card) in **Setup** → **SEO**, so search
engines and AI assistants can tell who runs the site and how to reach you:

- **Entity**: the business's name and description, its phone and email (with **Contact is
  for** set to *customer support*), its address, and what kind of business it is, from the
  style you picked (a plumber, a restaurant, a salon), with the areas it serves, its
  opening hours, its price range and its social profiles where you gave them. A personal
  site is published as a person.
- **Entity logo**: your site logo, when the site already has one.
- **AI agents**: when an assistant should use the site, and how to reach a person there.
  These open the site's guide for AI assistants, so an assistant asked about your business
  finds what it does, where it works and how to reach you.

The email and the street address also fill **Setup** → **Details** →
[**Business details**](../building-sites/bindings/overview.md#site-details), as the site's
support email and postal address.

Only empty fields are filled, so nothing you already entered there is replaced. Change any
of it in **Setup** → **SEO** at any time.

## A store from the start {#a-store-from-the-start}

On a paid plan, a site with the **Online store** style also gets three to six **products**,
each with a description and a photo, and a **Cart** button in the header. Each product gets
a suggested price for what it is, so it can be bought; review or change it, and add your own
photo, in **Products**. A product without a price shows **Price coming soon** on the store, with no
**Add to cart**, until you give it one. See [Product copy and catalogs with
AI](./products-with-ai.md).

When you describe what you sell as kinds of product, such as *"OEM and aftermarket parts,
tires, riding gear"*, and name no product, the first products are **samples**: a plain
item of each kind, such as *Knobby dirt bike tire*, at a suggested price. **Products**
marks each one **Sample**, and its editor asks you to replace it with your own product.
Saving your changes to a sample makes it yours. Your store shows them as ordinary products,
never with the word "sample". When you name the products you sell, those are the ones
made, as before.

The home page's **Newsletter sign-up** band is the store's own email sign-up field, which
adds subscribers to your contacts. Your contact form stays on the contact page.

It also gets the pages every store needs, ready to use and published with the rest of the
site. They do not count toward the pages you asked for:

- **Your account** at `/account`, where shoppers sign in or create an account, see their
  orders with status and tracking, keep their addresses, and find the items they saved
  with **Save** on a product page. User accounts are turned on for the site.
- **Your cart** at `/cart`, with checkout.

  These two are set to **Unlisted**, so search engines don't index them. The policy
  pages below are listed like the rest of your site.
- **Shipping & returns**, **Privacy policy** and **Terms of sale**, written as drafts. The
  facts only you know, such as your return window or where you ship from, are left in
  [brackets] for you to fill in. If your plan already has a shipping page, it is used
  instead of a second one.

The header links **Account** beside the cart, and the footer links your account and
policies. When the site is built, **Finish your store** lists what is left: connect
payments, review your products and prices, set shipping and tax, and fill in your
policies, each with a button to the page where you do it. If your workspace's Stripe
account already takes payments, the payments step reads **Payments connected** and is
marked done; if Stripe still needs details, it says what.

## A blog from the start {#a-blog-from-the-start}

A site with the **Blog & writing** style also gets a **Blog** collection with three posts
on a paid plan, or one post on the Free plan, signed with the business name and published
with the site. Each post has a cover photo, and the blog's index page shows the covers on
its cards. See
[Build a blog](../building-sites/site-templates/build-a-blog.md).

## Events from the start {#events-from-the-start}

When your business holds dated events, such as a venue, a club, a studio with classes, a
meetup, a church or a theater, and your workspace has the
[Event Calendar add-on](../content-and-data/events/overview.md), the start adds your first
three upcoming events to the **Events** page, each with a cover photo. They are dated after
the day you start, in your site's time zone, and a day or time you gave in your description
is kept as you gave it. The page about your events, or else your home page, lists them with
the **Event List**, in one of its planned sections.

The events are published, so they show on your site straight away, when you have the
admin or editor role on the site. With the author role they are saved as drafts, and the
job says so; someone with the editor role publishes them on the **Events** page. Without
the add-on the start adds no events. Change or delete them on the **Events** page like any
event.

## On the Free plan {#on-the-free-plan}

A Free start builds one or two pages: the home page and the one page your business most
needs. It keeps the site within about eight sections in all, uses the same look, header,
footer and contact form as a paid start, and drafts no welcome email or products. A blog
gets one first post.
It spends from the [300 AI credits](./ai-credits.md#free-plan) a Free workspace has each
month.

## Who can use it {#who-can-use-it}

**Start with AI** appears when your workspace has AI generation, you have the **Generate
with AI** permission, and AI is on for the site. Otherwise a new site opens on the starter
site. See [Who can use Aglyn AI](./overview.md#who-can-use-it).
