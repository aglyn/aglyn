# Create with AI on every list

:::note Beta
**Aglyn AI is in beta.** It is available today on every plan that includes it,
and it is still changing as we improve it. Beta means you can use it now, while
what it builds and the steps on this page may still change.
:::

Wherever Aglyn AI can make the thing a list holds, the list has a **Create with AI**
button with a sparkle icon, beside its own create button. An empty list shows it in its
empty state too, so the first item can be one you describe.

## The button {#the-button}

**Create with AI** opens a window with one question, such as **What is the page for?**,
and a box for your answer, up to 4,000 characters. Some lists ask one more thing beside it,
such as the page's type or the kind of email. Then:

1. Choose the window's plan button, such as **Plan the page**.
2. The job plans first. The window shows the plan, its estimated cost and **Confirm plan**.
   Nothing is built until you confirm it.
3. What it builds is a draft in the list you started from. The window's first button opens
   it.

You can close the window at any point; the job carries on in
[AI jobs](./ai-jobs-and-activity.md).

## Where it appears {#where-it-appears}

| List | What you describe | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Templates** | What each page made from the template shows, and what it is one page for: a collection entry, a product or an author | [Generate a page template](../building-sites/site-templates/templates-library.md#generate-a-page-template-with-aglyn-ai) |
| **Layouts** | A header, navigation and footer | [Generate a layout](../building-sites/screens-and-layouts/layouts.md#generate-a-layout-with-aglyn-ai) |

| **Marketing → Campaigns** | The campaign's email | [Generate an email](../marketing-and-automation/email-campaigns/generate-with-ai.md#where-to-start-it) |
| **Automation**, its actions and workflows, and the workspace's automations | What should happen, and when | [Automations with AI](./automations-with-ai.md) |
| **Logic → Functions and Variables** | A value to work out, or one to keep in one place | [Functions and variables with AI](./logic-with-ai.md) |

Blog posts, datasets and CRM records have no **Create with AI** button. In the CRM, AI
summarizes a record, drafts an email and matches import columns instead; see
[CRM by AI](./crm-by-ai.md). To create several of these things from one request, ask the
chat: see [Build from Assist chat](./assist-builds.md).

## What you get {#what-you-get}

Every button makes a **draft**: a page with no live address, a form that collects nothing
until you place it, an email nothing sends, an automation that is switched off. It uses
your theme and reuses what the site already has, following
[the building rules](./how-aglyn-ai-builds.md#the-building-rules), and reads your
[business profile](./business-profile.md).

## On every plan {#without-the-add-on}

On every paid plan, **Create with AI** runs on the AI credits your plan includes each
month; nothing else needs to be bought. An
[Aglyn AI tier](./ai-credits.md#the-aglyn-ai-add-on) adds more credits on top when you need
them, and an owner or admin chooses it in **Billing**.

A Free workspace's buttons work from the [300 AI credits](./ai-credits.md#free-plan) it
gets each month.

If Create with AI is switched off for your workspace, the button is still there and
opens a short window saying what it would make:

- an owner or admin sees **Add Aglyn AI**, which opens **Billing** to choose a tier, or
  **Not now**;
- anyone else is asked to have a workspace owner or admin choose one.

## When it is not there {#when-it-is-not-there}

**Create with AI** is hidden when you lack the **Generate with AI** permission, or when AI
is [switched off for the site](./overview.md#switch-ai-off-for-one-site).
