# Phone

:::caution Rolling out
Phone is a **release-flagged feature**. It is turned on one workspace at a
time while it rolls out. If you do not see **Phone** in your workspace's tabs,
it is not on for your workspace yet.
:::

Phone gives your workspace its own business number. Customers call and text
it, your team answers from Aglyn, and every text and call is kept with the
customer's record in the CRM.

## What it costs

Phone is an add-on on the **Starter** and **Pro** plans: **$15 a month**.
It includes:

- one business number, local or toll-free;
- **300 minutes** of calls a month, in or out, voicemail included;
- **300 texts** a month, sent or received.

Past those, minutes and texts are billed at cost + 30%, on the same usage
line as storage and bandwidth. Turn it on under **Billing → Add-ons → Phone**.

## Choose your number

Open **Phone → Setup**.

1. Pick **Local** or **Toll-free**. For a local number, type the area code you
   want and choose **Find numbers**.
2. Pick a number from the list.
3. Choose the **site it answers for**. Your saved replies link to that site.
4. Give it a name for the inbox, such as *Front desk*, and choose **Use**.

Only owners and admins can choose, move or release the number.

### Keep a number you already have

Under **Move a number you already have**, choose **Move a number** and enter the
details exactly as your current carrier's bill shows them: the account holder,
the account number, the transfer PIN and the service address. Moving a number
usually takes one to three weeks. Calls keep reaching you at your current
carrier until the day it moves, and the phone company emails you as it goes.

## Register your number for texting

US carriers only deliver business texts from a registered number. Fill in
**Texting registration** on **Setup** and choose **Submit registration**.

- **A local number** is registered as your business with the carriers (A2P
  10DLC). It usually takes one to two weeks. You need your business's EIN.
- **A toll-free number** is verified instead, usually within a few business
  days. Sole proprietors without an EIN should choose a toll-free number.

Use **Check status** to see where it stands. Calls work while you wait.

## Texts

**Phone → Inbox** lists every conversation with your number, newest first.
Pick one to read it and reply. **New text** starts a conversation with a
number that has not texted you yet.

- **Saved replies** under the message box fill in your site's address and
  name. Write your own on **Settings**, for example one with your booking page
  or waiver link pasted in.
- When the number belongs to someone in your CRM, the conversation shows their
  name, and each text is added to their timeline.
- A customer who texts **STOP** is not texted again until they text **START**.
- **Archive** tidies a finished conversation away; a new text brings it back.

## Calls

During your business hours, a call rings the **cell phones** you list on
**Settings**, all at once. The first person to answer takes it, and they see
the caller's number. If nobody answers, or the call comes outside your hours,
the caller hears your greeting and can leave a voicemail.

**Phone → Calls** lists every call. A voicemail shows as text once it is
written out. With **Text a missed caller** set, Phone texts a caller back
after a call nobody answered.

### Call a customer from your business number

On **Calls**, choose **Call**, enter the number and your own cell. Phone rings
your cell first; when you answer, it calls the customer, and they see your
business number.

## Settings

- **Business hours**, in your time zone. With no days ticked, your number is
  always open.
- **Ring these cell phones**: up to five numbers.
- **Seconds to ring before voicemail**: 10 to 60.
- **Voicemail greeting** and **Closed message**, read aloud to callers.
- **Write out voicemails as text**.
- **Text a missed caller**.
- **Saved replies**.

## Recording

Only voicemail is recorded, after the caller hears your greeting. A call your
team answers is never recorded.
