# Enterprise and the Custom plan

**Custom** is the plan for enterprise agreements, and the only plan that is not
self-serve. It is quoted rather than listed, and it is the plan the pages in
this section describe. Until November 2026 it was labeled **Enterprise**; only
the name changed.

Custom carries everything in Pro, plus [white-label](../workspace-and-billing/white-label.md),
single sign-on, a dedicated support commitment, no transaction fee, and limits
sized by your agreement.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| [Single sign-on](./sso.md) | SAML SSO against your identity provider, optionally enforced so passwords stop working. |
| [Support commitments](./support-tiers.md) | A **24–48 hour** first-response commitment measured in clock hours, and a named success manager. |
| [Availability & status](./uptime-and-status.md) | What we measure, what [/status](/status) shows, and what we have **not** committed to. |
| [Security & compliance](./security-and-compliance.md) | The material a security review needs, including what we do not have. |

## What Custom does not change

Worth saying plainly, because it is the usual assumption:

- **Quotas and features** are entitlements, not plan names. A Custom
  workspace can have any combination of limits, and they are set per
  organization rather than implied by the plan.
- **The product** is the same product. Custom is about how we contract,
  respond and authenticate — not a separate build.

## Getting the Custom plan

Contact us — Custom is the one plan with no self-serve checkout, so the
plan itself is arranged with us rather than bought from the billing page.

Everything after that is yours to configure. [Single sign-on](./sso.md) in
particular is set up by your own admins in **Organization → Settings → Single
sign-on**: claim your domain, verify a DNS TXT record, exchange IdP metadata,
rehearse enforcement, and activate. There is no provisioning step on our side
and nothing to wait for.
