# Working in the Aglyn console

The console is at `https://app.aglyn.com`. It is where a person designs pages
and changes settings. Use it only through a browser tool the person has given
you, in their signed-in session. If they are signed out, ask them to sign in;
never type a password.

## Before you click

- Prefer a connector tool when one exists. It is faster and leaves a cleaner
  trail. The console is for what the tools cannot do.
- Say what you are about to change and where, then do it. Ask before anything
  that goes live, costs money, emails customers, or cannot be undone:
  publishing, plan or billing changes, deleting, sending.
- Read the screen before acting. Labels and layouts change; trust what is in
  front of you over what you remember.

## Finding your way

- Pick the workspace, then the site. Site work lives under that site: its
  pages, media, and a **Setup** area for business details, SEO, domains and
  integrations.
- Workspace-wide things (team, billing, API keys and connected apps) live in
  the workspace's **Settings**.
- A **?** beside a page title opens the matching help page on
  `docs.aglyn.com`. Read it when a screen is unfamiliar.

## Editing pages (the Besigner)

Pages are edited visually in Aglyn's editor, the Besigner.

- Select an element, then change it with its fields: content and options
  under its attributes, appearance under its style groups, colors from the
  theme's color pickers. Use the editor's own device switcher to check phone
  and tablet layouts; do not resize the browser window.
- **Never write code to get a look.** No CSS, no JSON, no style snippets. If
  a look cannot be reached with the fields on screen, tell the person what is
  missing instead of working around it.
- Site-wide looks come from the theme: a built-in look plus the brand colors
  and fonts. Change those there, not element by element.
- Something that repeats across pages should be a reusable component, not a
  copy on each page.
- Type in short bursts and let the editor settle between edits.
- Changes are a draft until the site is published. Preview, then ask before
  publishing.

## When you are done

Tell the person what changed, on which site and page, and whether it is
published or still a draft.
